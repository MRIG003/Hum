How Engage handles data

A short, plain description of what Engage collects and who can see it. This is a summary for people joining a meeting, not a legal agreement.

What the host can see

  • How many people joined and how many took part.
  • Group results for polls, pulse checks, recall checks, word clouds and reactions. Answers are never shown next to names.
  • Your questions, with your name unless you post anonymously.

What nobody can see

  • Who is idle, quiet, speaking, or looking at another window. Engage does not measure these.
  • Your points, bingo card and predictions.
  • Your notes and action items. They stay in your browser and are never sent to Engage.

What Engage does not collect

  • No audio, video, screen content or transcripts.
  • No face, voice or behaviour analysis.
  • No accounts for attendees. You can join with a first name or anonymously.

How long data is kept

During this pilot, meeting data is held in the server's memory and removed 24 hours after the meeting ends, or sooner if the server restarts. A planned release will keep meeting data for 30 days and let hosts delete it at any time.

The mini window

Engage can open a small floating window. If you share your entire screen in a meeting, the audience can see that window. Share a single window or tab instead, or press "I'm presenting" to close it.

Questions

Ask the person who invited you to the meeting. They can tell you who is running this pilot.
