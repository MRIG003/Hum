How Engage handles data

A short, plain description of what Engage collects and who can see it. This is a summary for people joining a meeting, not a legal agreement.

What the host can see

  • Your name, as you entered it. Please use your real name, as it appears in the meeting.
  • What you did in Engage and when: reactions, questions, whether you answered each prompt, and how long you have gone without any activity in Engage.
  • Whether you marked yourself as presenting.
  • Group results for polls, pulse checks, recall checks, word clouds and reactions. Your actual answers, including “Missed that”, are never shown next to your name.
  • Your questions, shown with your name to the host and the room.

What nobody can see

  • Whether you are listening, speaking, or looking at another window. Engage only sees actions you take inside Engage, so a quiet spell does not mean you were not paying attention.
  • Your points and game scores. Games show each person only their own score; the host sees how many people got each round right, never who.
  • Your notes and action items. They stay in your browser and are never sent to Engage.

What Engage does not collect

  • No audio, video, screen content or transcripts.
  • No face, voice or attention analysis.
  • No accounts for attendees. You join with your name.

How long data is kept

Meeting data is saved on the server so a restart does not lose a meeting in progress. Access tokens are stored only as one-way hashes. Meetings started without a host account are deleted 24 hours after they end. Meetings that belong to a host account are kept for 30 days. The host can delete a meeting, and everything in it, at any time.

Hosts can optionally create an account with an email address and password, to keep their past meetings together. Attendees never need an account. The CSV export contains group results and question text, never names.

Website analytics

The public home page, and only that page, may use Microsoft Clarity to count visits and show how people use the page (for example clicks and scrolling). Clarity is never loaded inside Google Meet, in a meeting, or on the demo and host pages, and it never sees anything you type or do in Engage. By default Clarity masks text typed into form fields. See Microsoft's privacy statement for how Clarity handles its data.

The public demo

The demo at /try creates a throwaway meeting with a made-up participant. It is never saved and is deleted 30 minutes after it starts.

The mini window

Engage can open a small floating window. If you share your entire screen in a meeting, the audience can see that window. Share a single window or tab instead, or press "I'm presenting" to close it.

Questions

Ask the person who invited you to the meeting. They can tell you who is running this pilot.
