How it works

Up and running in under a minute

  1. 1

    Open Engage in Meet

    In your call, choose Activities, then Engage, and press Start Engage.

  2. 2

    Everyone gets the panel

    Invite the call with one button. People join with their name. No accounts.

  3. 3

    Ask, react, play

    Pick a tool, launch, and watch the room respond. End with a coach summary.

Questions

Do participants need to install anything?

In Google Meet they open Engage from Activities. Anyone can also join with the link in a browser. Participants never need an account.

Is anything recorded?

No audio, video, screen or transcript. Engage only sees what people do inside Engage, such as answering a poll or sending a reaction.

Who sees who has been quiet?

Only the host. Participants are told exactly what the host can see before they join.

Are game scores shared?

No. Each person sees only their own score. The host sees how many people got each round right, never who.
