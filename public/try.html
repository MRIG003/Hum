Try Engage in your browser

You are the host on the left and a participant on the right, both wired to a real, throwaway meeting. No sign-up, no Meet call. It is deleted when the timer ends.

  1. On the left, open Host tools and choose Ask the room. Write a question, add two options and press Launch.
  2. On the right, the question fills the participant's panel. Answer it, then watch the host side update.
  3. Send a reaction or a question from the right, and see the host get a cue on the left.
