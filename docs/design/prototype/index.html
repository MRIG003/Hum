Design notes

  • Polls, floor invitations and games only add a dot to the pill.
  • Only the recall check may open the pill, and never while presenting or in Focus.
  • While presenting, nothing shows and nothing queues. Skipped prompts are only counted.
  • "Missed that" is a normal answer, shown separately from wrong answers.
  • No sound and no notifications anywhere.

Host console additions
