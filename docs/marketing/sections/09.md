### The time budget

None of this works if marketing only gets the time left over after building. A suggested split for a solo founder in Q1: **about 50% of working hours on customers and distribution, about 50% on product**. Most of the product half is the activation work above (hosting, templates, counters, emails), so the split is less painful than it sounds. Pilots and interviews count as both. Confirm or change this in §13 (open decision #4).
