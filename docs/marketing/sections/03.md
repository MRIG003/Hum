## 3. Current state

### Team composition (marketing surface area)

| Person | Role | Marketing surface area |
|---|---|---|
| Founder | Product, engineering, design, everything | All of it: positioning, website, Marketplace listing, outreach, content, support |
| Claude Code + the marketing-skills library (installed in `.claude/skills/`) | Execution layer | Drafting copy, research, competitor pages, email flows, listing optimization, this plan |

**Gaps:** no one owns customer conversations or distribution yet, and the founder's time is the only budget. The founder is builder-first: deep in product and engineering, with marketing as a learned skill. This is normal at this stage. A first marketing hire is not on the table until revenue exists (§10). When it comes, it should be a π-shaped Product Marketing + Content Lead, part-time or contract, titled Lead, not Head or VP.

### Marketing budget (current)

- **Paid acquisition:** $0
- **Tooling:** Render hosting (free tier, which is why the app sleeps when idle), Resend email (free tier), Microsoft Clarity (free), Gmail. About $0/month.
- **Retainers / contractors:** none
- **Headcount:** founder time only. Hours per week for marketing are not yet set (open decision #4).
- **Blended CAC:** not measurable. No customers. Once hosts arrive, CAC = founder hours × an hourly value the founder sets, plus tools. Not paid ad spend.
- **Spend as % of ARR:** not applicable. ARR is $0.

**Tier:** Tier 1, pre-seed / bootstrapped, at the floor of the range. **Implication:** every move in the 90-day plan must be doable by one person with free tools, plus at most about $25/month of essentials (a domain, an always-on server, and a domain email). Those three items are the only spend this plan asks for (open decision #3).

### Phase of SaaS growth

**$0 ARR, before product-market fit.** This is earlier than the "$0 to $10K, grueling" phase. Engage has not yet had a single outside host run a real meeting. The binding constraint is **evidence**, not traffic. Until three hosts say "I'd use this again" (the product plan's own Phase 0 exit test), more acquisition is wasted. The first 30 days are about pilots, not channels.

**Shape: "strong product, weak everything else."** The product side is unusually complete for a team of one, and the voice is already good.
