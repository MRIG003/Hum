## 8. Revenue

### Current state

Engage is free and listed as free on the Marketplace. There is no Stripe account, no pricing page, and no stated intent to charge. That's right for today. Charging before 10 hosts use it weekly would test the price, not the product. But a bootstrapped founder needs a dated plan for revenue, or "free" quietly becomes the business model.

### The plan

**Move 1 — Keep the core free, permanently.**
Everything that happens inside a meeting, for meetings up to about 50 people, stays free: reactions, Q&A, polls, pulse checks, recall checks, Focus moment, the summary. This is the acquisition engine (every meeting is a demo), and it's what the Marketplace listing promises. Don't take it back later.

**Move 2 — Find out what trainers would pay for (weeks 5–12).**
In every design-partner interview from week 5 on, ask four questions taken from the `pricing` skill's willingness-to-pay method: at what price would this be too expensive to consider; expensive but worth it; a bargain; so cheap you'd doubt it. Then ask what they pay for Mentimeter, Slido or similar today, and who pays (them, their employer, or their client). Ten answers are enough to set a first price. *Skill:* `pricing`, `customer-research`.

**Move 3 — A fake door in Q2.**
Once history across meetings ships (§6 Move 2), add a calm "Pro: coming soon. Tell me when it's ready" link next to it on the summary and the My meetings page. Count clicks per active host. This measures real intent without charging anyone. *Skills:* `paywalls`, `ab-testing`.

**Move 4 — Launch Pro in Q3, gated on evidence.**
Launch only if: **25+ weekly active hosts**, **40%+ six-week retention**, and **at least 5 hosts who said they'd pay** in Move 2. A working hypothesis to test, not a decision:

| Plan | Price (hypothesis) | For | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Anyone | Everything in-meeting, up to 50 attendees, last 3 summaries |
| Pro | $9–12/host/month, or ~$96/year | Trainers, facilitators, frequent hosts | Meeting history and trends, unlimited summaries and CSV, templates library, up to 200 attendees, remove the Engage mark, shareable session results |
| Team | Later (Q4+) | L&D teams, departments | Shared templates, org-level trends, admin, SSO (Phase 3) |

The price sits just under Mentimeter Basic ($11.99/presenter/month billed yearly) and Slido's Engage plan ($17.50/month). That's deliberate: in a known category, you don't have to explain the price, only why you're better for this use. *Skills:* `pricing`, `paywalls`. *Tool:* Stripe (to set up in Q3; not wired today).

**Move 5 — The L&D team deal (Q4).**
When 3+ trainers from one company use Engage, offer that company's L&D lead a simple team plan billed annually. The sales material needed is small: a one-page privacy and security summary (most of it exists in `privacy.html` and the principles test), a one-page "what Engage shows, and what it never shows", and a results example. *Skills:* `sales-enablement`, `revops` (only once there's a pipeline worth tracking).

**Skipped:**
- **Lifetime deals (AppSumo-style):** fast cash, but they fill the product with bargain-hunters, permanently cap revenue per user, and don't match the calm, trustworthy voice.
- **Usage-based pricing (per attendee):** punishes hosts for growing their meetings, which is the behaviour Engage wants. Keep it per host.
- **Charging attendees:** never.

### Unit economics

| Metric | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| ARPC (avg monthly revenue per customer) | TBD | $0 today. Hypothesis: $8–10 blended after annual discounts |
| Blended CAC | TBD | Founder hours × hourly value + tools. Measure from week 1 by logging hours per channel |
| Annual retention rate | TBD | No paying customers. Proxy: 6-week host retention (§6) |
| LTV (rough) | TBD | ARPC × 12 ÷ annual churn |
| LTV / CAC | TBD | Health benchmark above 3. Only meaningful after Q3 |

All five are open. Blended CAC is the most important of them (§13, open decision #1). Even with $0 of paid spend, CAC isn't zero. It's paid in founder hours, and those are the scarcest resource in the company.

### 12-month revenue outlook

- **Q1:** $0. Price evidence from 10 conversations.
- **Q2:** $0. Fake door live. Stripe account opened, nothing charged.
- **Q3:** Pro launches if the gates in Move 4 are met. First revenue. A realistic range is $100–500 MRR by quarter end.
- **Q4:** $500–1,500 MRR if the recap loop and Marketplace work. First team deal in conversation. These are hypotheses with wide error bars, not forecasts.
