## 1. Executive summary

**What this plan optimizes for:** proof that trainers and facilitators come back to Engage week after week, with one founder, under $20 a month, and no paid ads.

Engage is built, and as of late October 2026 it looks the part. It has a new logo and visual design, a homepage with a playable demo and real screenshots, its own address (`engage.neuronlab.in`) on always-on hosting, and a Marketplace submission package ready for Google. What it still doesn't have is a single outside host. So the first 90 days aren't about traffic. They're about ten real hosts running real sessions and coming back. Every channel after that depends on what those ten teach us.

The new homepage line sets the frame for everything else: **"Every voice in the meeting, not just the loudest few."**

### Three big bets

**1. Trainers and facilitators first, not "everyone who runs meetings."**
Of the four audiences Engage could serve, trainers and facilitators have the biggest problem and feel it most often. The session *is* their product, they run several a week, and many already pay $10–25 a month for Mentimeter or Slido themselves. Engage's sharpest features were made for them: seeing who has gone quiet, the recall check with "Missed that", one-minute games to wake the room up, and the coach summary. Team leads come second (Q3), educators later, webinar hosts not this year.

**2. Distribution through the Google Workspace Marketplace and through the meetings themselves.**
The Marketplace is where Meet users already look for add-ons. Inside Meet, any participant who doesn't have Engage installed is invited to install it when the host starts it. That's a referral loop built into the platform. On top of that, a calm line on the attendee recap and a 60-second demo video (made with Google Flow plus real screen recordings) turn every session and every post into a demo. With no ad budget, these channels can grow without growing the founder's hours. Hand-recruited pilots start them.

**3. Free in the meeting, paid for what happens after.**
Everything inside a meeting stays free, which is the promise on the listing and the engine of the loop. Pro, priced just under Mentimeter at a hypothesized $9–12 per host per month, adds meeting history and trends, unlimited summaries and exports, larger meetings and shareable results. Pro launches only when the evidence says so: 25 weekly active hosts, 40% six-week retention, and five hosts who said they'd pay.

### What twelve months looks like, plausibly

- An approved Marketplace listing with 10+ honest reviews and a demo video
- About 120 weekly active hosts, mostly trainers and facilitators, with team leads arriving through attendee spillover
- The Meet install invitation, the recap line and Marketplace search as the three biggest sources of new hosts
- Pro live since Q3, with $500–1,500 MRR by month 12. This is a hypothesis with wide error bars, not a forecast.
- A measured cost per host, in hours and dollars, for every channel
- A clear answer on whether to stay bootstrapped or raise, based on growth and retention rather than hope

### 90-day priorities

1. **Ship the Marketplace submission (this week):** verify `neuronlab.in` in Search Console, publish the new package, and fix any rejection within 24 hours. Move the developer contact off Gmail onto the domain.
2. **Make the demo video:** one 60–75 second hybrid demo (Flow for the people, real recordings of `/try` for the product), plus a 15-second vertical cut. Kit in `docs/marketing/demo-video-flow.md`.
3. **Run 3 pilot meetings by week 4 and reach 10 active hosts by week 12,** through 25+ personal invitations and concierge first sessions.
4. **Measure what matters:** privacy-safe aggregate counters, the feedback survey, and a north star of *Weekly Engaged Meetings*.
5. **Start the compounding assets:** the attendee recap line, founder-led LinkedIn with the video, 6 honest comparison and how-to pages, and directory listings.
6. **Launch once, when it's earned:** Product Hunt and Show HN in week 11 or later, only with Marketplace approval and three hosts willing to be quoted.
