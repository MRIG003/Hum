## 11. Marketing operations stack

### The thesis

One founder can't run eight channels by hand. One founder with the marketing-skills library and a few connected tools can cover the drafting, research and analysis that would otherwise need a content writer, a lifecycle marketer, an SEO person and an analyst. The founder keeps what can't be delegated: the conversations with hosts, the judgment about what's true, and the voice.

The skills are already installed in this repo (`.claude/skills/`, 52 skills from `coreyhaines31/marketingskills`). Every session working on Engage can call them by name.

### A concrete example

The stack has already been tested once on Engage. When Google rejected the Marketplace listing on 15 October 2026 (trademark attribution and screenshots), the fix went out the next day as a single commit. The founder rewrote the listing, made five new 1280×800 real-app screenshots and added the terms and support pages, working with Claude Code and with no outside help. That's the pattern for the whole plan: the founder decides, the skills draft, and everything ships as a commit.

**The demonstration for week 1:** run `prospecting` + `cold-email` to build the list of 40 trainers and draft 10 personal invitations in one session, then send them by hand. If that takes under two hours, the stack thesis holds for outreach.

### Capability unlocks by funding stage

| Stage | Headcount | Tooling | Channels live |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Now: bootstrapped, ~$10/month** | Founder + Claude + skills | Render (always-on), Resend, Clarity, Search Console, GitHub MCP | Marketplace, personal outreach, LinkedIn, communities, SEO content, directories, recap loop |
| **First revenue ($500+ MRR)** | + a contract writer or designer for a few hours a month | + Stripe, a keyword data tool (when content proves itself) | + one paid search test, launch platforms, a data story for L&D media |
| **Seed (if raised; see §10)** | + first marketing hire: π-shaped Product Marketing + Content Lead | + product analytics, CRM for team deals | + paid search and LinkedIn tests ($5–15K/month), events and speaking |
| **Series A** | + growth marketer, designer | Full stack | Paid at scale, Zoom and Teams marketplaces, team and enterprise sales |

### Team and agency model (RACI)

| Function | Owned by (strategy) | Executed by |
|---|---|---|
| Growth marketing (demand engine) | Founder | Founder + Claude skills; contractors for design at first revenue |
| Product marketing (story engine) | Founder | Founder + `product-marketing`, `copywriting` |
| Content marketing (trust engine) | Founder | Claude drafts, founder edits every piece for voice and truth; contract writer at first revenue |

No hire is recommended in the first 90 days. The first hire question becomes real only at the seed decision point in §10.
