## 10. 12-month outlook

### Framing

**Budget method.** Neither standard method fits a company with $0 ARR and $0 spend. Revenue-based budgeting (5–40% of ARR) gives $0. Goal-based budgeting needs a CAC, and there isn't one yet. So for the first two quarters, the budget is **time**, not money. Cash spend is limited to essentials:

| Line | Monthly | Annual |
|---|---|---|
| Always-on hosting (Render Starter, check current price) | ~$7 | ~$84 |
| Domain | ~$1 | ~$15 |
| Email on the domain (forwarding: free; a Workspace mailbox if wanted: ~$7) | $0–7 | $0–84 |
| **Total essentials** | **~$8–15** | **~$100–185** |
| Experimental buffer (+20%, held for a Q3 paid search test or a directory fee) | — | ~$300 |

**Goal-based math, for when revenue exists (Q3 onward).** Illustrative only. Every input is a hypothesis to replace with real data:
- Target by month 12: $1,000 MRR = $12,000 ARR
- ARPC hypothesis: $9/month → **about 110 paying hosts**
- If 10% of weekly active hosts pay, that needs **about 1,100 weekly active hosts**. That's a big jump from 10 in Q1, and is the honest reason to treat the $1,000 MRR figure as a stretch, not a forecast.
- Formula from the `marketing-plan` skill: `[(New ARR / (ARPC × 12)) × CAC] / annual retention`. With CAC in founder hours, this tells you how many hours each paying host costs. That is the number that decides whether to keep going organic-only.

**Growth pattern expected.** Early, slow and linear at first: hosts added one by one through outreach, then small steps up when the Marketplace listing goes live, at the launch, and when the recap loop starts working. No hockey stick. The plan layers S-curves in this order: **hand-recruited pilots → Marketplace search → attendee recap loop → search content**, starting each before the last one flattens.

**Resource split (70/20/10 of marketing time):** 70% on what's working now (outreach and pilots in Q1), 20% on the next curve (Marketplace and content), 10% on experiments (one community, one new format).

### Q1 — Months 1–3

**Funding state:** Tier 1, bootstrapped, about $10/month spend.

**Focus:** Prove that trainers come back.

**Outcomes by end of Q1:**
- Domain live, always-on hosting, Marketplace approved
- 3 pilot meetings run (Phase 0 exit test passed or failed, honestly)
- 10 active hosts, at least 6 still active in week 12
- 10 interviews recorded, customer language captured in `.agents/product-marketing.md`
- Counters live; first activation and retention reads
- Positioning decision made and applied everywhere

**KPI targets:** 10 weekly active hosts; attendee participation rate above 70%; "would use again" above 70%; distraction "yes" below 10%; 3 Marketplace reviews.

**S-curve position:** Outreach curve growing. Marketplace curve being staged.

### Q2 — Months 4–6

**Funding state:** Tier 1. Still bootstrapped.

**Focus:** From hand-recruited to found.

**Outcomes by end of Q2:**
- Product Hunt / Show HN launch done (if not in Q1)
- Recap loop and Marketplace search are each a top-3 source of new hosts
- Meeting history across sessions shipped (if pilots asked for it)
- 6–10 content pages indexed; directories done
- Fake-door Pro interest measured
- Team-lead templates added as the second audience starts arriving

**KPI targets:** 25 weekly active hosts; 40% of new hosts from non-outreach sources; activation rate above 50% (closed windows); 10 Marketplace reviews.

**S-curve position:** Outreach plateaus by design (founder time is fixed). Marketplace and recap loop growing. Content staged.

### Q3 — Months 7–9

**Funding state:** Tier 1, with first revenue if the Pro gates are met.

**Focus:** First revenue without losing the free product's reach.

**Outcomes by end of Q3:**
- Pro launched (if gates in §8 are met); Stripe wired
- Shareable session results and template sharing live
- First paid search test ($300 cap, "Google Meet polls" terms) to measure CAC against Pro revenue
- First data story from aggregate meeting data ("where meetings dip"), pitched to L&D media
- A decision on the next platform (Zoom Apps or Teams) based on where non-Meet hosts come from

**KPI targets:** 60 weekly active hosts; 15–40 paying hosts; $150–500 MRR; 6-week retention above 40%.

**S-curve position:** Recap loop and Marketplace growing. Content curve starting to grow. Paid staged as an experiment only.

### Q4 — Months 10–12

**Funding state:** Tier 1. Self-funded from revenue, or a decision point on raising (see below).

**Focus:** Compound what works; open the second audience.

**Outcomes by end of Q4:**
- Team leads at Workspace companies reached through Marketplace and attendee spillover
- First L&D team deal in conversation
- Org-level trends prototype (start of Phase 3)
- Second platform add-on started, if Q3 data supports it
- Marketing plan v2 written from a year of real data

**KPI targets:** 120 weekly active hosts; $500–1,500 MRR; blended CAC measured (hours and dollars) for every channel; LTV/CAC estimate with 6+ months of data.

**S-curve position:** Marketplace may start plateauing. Content and the team-lead audience are the next curves.

### When to reconsider funding

This plan assumes bootstrapping. Revisit that if, by the end of Q2, **weekly active hosts are growing 15%+ month over month with retention above 40%**. That is the signal that money would buy speed rather than hide a weak product. At that point the next tier (seed, $5–15K/month of paid tests and a first marketing hire) becomes a real option, and the capability-unlocks table in §11 applies. If growth is slower than that, stay bootstrapped. The product's costs are low enough to allow it.
