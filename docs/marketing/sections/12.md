## 12. Tactical idea bank

Sections 4–8 say what Engage is *doing*. This section maps what's *possible*: all 139 ideas from the `marketing-ideas` skill, each tagged to an AARRR stage and given an Engage-specific status. Four ideas serve two stages and appear twice (#79, #91, #114, #117), so the tables hold 143 rows.

The skip list matters as much as the rest. It records what Engage is deliberately not doing, and why, so nobody has to argue it again.

### Status legend

- **Now (Q1):** in the 90-day plan, or can run alongside it with no extra capacity
- **Q2:** layer in after pilots and the foundation
- **Q3+:** after first revenue or after the Pro launch
- **Q4+:** long game
- **Skip / off-brand:** conflicts with the calm, privacy-first voice, the bootstrapped budget, or the business model

### Idea-bank summary

- **116** ideas map to Acquisition. That's normal for a company with no users yet: almost every tactic is about being found.
- **8** to Activation, **8** to Retention, **5** to Referral, **2** to Revenue, **2** cross-cutting, **2** developer-specific.
- By unique idea (139): **26 Now, 14 Q2, 25 Q3+, 26 Q4+, 48 Skip.**
- **Why 48 skips:** no paid budget (most ad channels), the privacy stance (pixel sharing, retargeting), the calm voice (giveaways, stunts, controversy, engagement pods), and fit (developer, hardware and enterprise-only tactics).

**What this shows:** the 90-day plan uses about 19% of the available tactical surface (26 of 139). That is the right amount for one person. The plan doesn't fail for lack of ideas. It fails if the founder spreads across 40 of them. As hosts, revenue and help arrive, this table is the backlog to draw from, in status order.
